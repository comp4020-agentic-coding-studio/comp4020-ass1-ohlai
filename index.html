Lights out.

At a Formula 1 start, the driver is doing the one thing in the sport you can also do: seeing a light go out and moving. Everything else — the braking, the tyre management, the car on the limit through Eau Rouge — is beyond you. This isn't. Find out by how little.

Hold, then release

Hold the space bar — or press and hold this pad on a touchscreen. The lights come on one by one. When all five go out, let go.

Where the numbers come from

Only one number on this page is measured: yours. The bands are reported ranges, not a measured distribution of named drivers — F1's per-start telemetry isn't published, and the figures circulating online trace back to reaction-test sites that cite nothing and disagree with each other.

So this page shows a band with its uncertainty visible rather than a bell curve that would look authoritative and be invented. Full provenance, including what could not be sourced, is in data/sources.md in the repository.
